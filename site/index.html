THREE SIMPLE STEPS

Speak. Finish.
Keep writing.

Use the floating widget or Option-Space.
Your text ends up on the clipboard.

  1. 1

    Pick your place

    Click the text field where you want to write, in a document, message, or editor.

  2. 2

    Say what's on your mind

    Press ⌥ Space to start. Speak, then press it again to finish. ⌥ Esc cancels.

  3. 3

    Make the words yours

    Dhwani copies the transcript. Paste with ⌘ V, or allow Accessibility for automatic paste into the original field.

Change apps or fields during dictation? Automatic paste is withheld. Your text is still on the clipboard.

SPEECH & PRIVACY

Your voice.
Your choices.

Dhwani uses Apple's speech engine and requires an available on-device language model. It has no cloud-recognition fallback.

These requirements are reviewed in source. Runtime network activity has not been independently audited.

On-device, or it doesn't start

Dictation replaces your clipboard. Other apps can read it, and pasted text is handled by the app receiving it.

GOOD TO KNOW

A few questions
before you start.

Still curious? Explore the project on GitHub ↗

Which Macs can use Dhwani?+

The beta is built for Apple Silicon Macs, M1 or newer, and targets macOS 14 or later. Your chosen language needs an available Apple on-device speech model. Live dictation on macOS 14 still needs acceptance testing. Intel Macs, Windows, and Linux are not supported by this build.

Is Dhwani free and open source?+

Yes. The beta is free to download, and the app source is MIT licensed. Apple's recognition engine and models are supplied by macOS. You can browse the code, contribute, or build it yourself.

Do I need Accessibility access?+

Only for automatic paste. Microphone and Speech Recognition access are required for dictation. Without Accessibility, Dhwani copies your transcript and you paste it with Command-V. Switching apps or fields blocks automatic paste.

Why might I need Open Anyway?+

This beta is locally signed and has not been notarized by Apple. If macOS blocks the first launch, Open Anyway may be available for Dhwani in System Settings > Privacy & Security. Some managed Macs do not allow it. Read the installation guide before starting.

What does the early beta mean?+

Live dictation, long recordings, cross-app paste, and clean-Mac installation still need full acceptance testing. There is no published recognition-accuracy benchmark. Review the completed checks and their limits, try it with non-sensitive text, and share feedback.
