First-launch help +

This beta hasn't gone through Apple's app verification process. Dictation and installation on a fresh Mac still need broader testing.

  1. Open the Dhwani copy in Applications.

A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Is Dhwani
right for you?

What you need to know
before your first try.

Why use Dhwani instead of Mac's built-in dictation?+

Choose Dhwani if you want a separate window for your spoken text, with Copy and optional local history. Both use Apple's speech recognition. Dhwani has no established accuracy advantage.

Which Macs and languages does it support?+

Macs with M1 or newer and macOS 14+. Intel Macs are not supported. Choose a language in Settings; Dhwani tells you if it can't run on your Mac. Live dictation on macOS 14 still needs testing.

Does it rewrite or polish what I say?+

It turns speech into text, with automatic punctuation. It doesn't use an AI writer to rewrite, summarize, or change your tone. Make edits in your message or document.

Should I expect to make corrections?+

Yes, especially to names and numbers. We're still testing different languages, microphones, and recording lengths. No accuracy rate has been established.

Can I use it without automatic paste?+

Yes. Leave Accessibility off and paste with Command-V. If you allow automatic paste, the original app and text box must still be focused.

What happens to my voice and saved text?+

Dhwani requests speech processing on your Mac; internet activity during use is still unverified. Read the speech and privacy details before allowing access. History starts off on a fresh install. Saved history isn't encrypted by Dhwani, and upgrades keep your settings.

What should I expect from the early beta?+

Try short, non-private text first. Dictation, automatic paste, permission recovery, long recordings, and fresh-Mac setup still need broader testing. Updates are manual: quit Dhwani and replace it in Applications with the new download. Beta details.
