HOW IT WORKS

From speaking
to pasted text.

Use the widget or the keyboard shortcut.
Choose whether Dhwani pastes for you.

  1. 1

    Choose where to write

    Click the text box in the message, note, or document you want to write.

  2. 2

    Press, speak, finish

    Press ⌥ Space to start. Speak, then press it again to finish. ⌥ Esc cancels.

  3. 3

    Paste your words

    Dhwani copies your text. Press ⌘ V to paste it yourself, or allow Accessibility so Dhwani can paste into the original text box.

Switched to another app or text box? Dhwani leaves your words copied so you can paste them yourself.

SPEECH & PRIVACY

You choose
what gets kept.

Dhwani uses Apple's speech recognition and asks it to work on your Mac. If your chosen language can't work that way, recording stays off.

We still need to independently check internet connections during use. Read the privacy details before allowing speech access.

Speech & privacy details ↗
01

History starts off

Dhwani only keeps a saved dictation history if you turn it on. Saved history stays on your Mac.

02

Sending is your choice

Dhwani doesn't press Return to send a message unless you turn that option on.

03

Copying replaces what you last copied

Your finished text goes to the clipboard. As with anything you copy, other apps may be able to read it.

BEFORE YOU DOWNLOAD

Is Dhwani
right for you?

Here's what it does, what you need,
and what we're still testing.

Why use Dhwani instead of Mac's built-in dictation?+

Dhwani gives you a separate floating widget where you can see and copy your spoken words. You can also choose to keep a local history. It uses Apple's speech recognition too. We haven't established that it is more accurate than Mac's built-in dictation.

Will it work on my Mac?+

You need a Mac with an M1 chip or newer and macOS 14 or later. Macs with Intel chips are not supported. Your chosen speech language also needs to be available for use on your Mac. Live dictation on macOS 14 still needs testing.

Which languages can I use?+

Choose a language in Dhwani's settings. The choices come from Apple's speech service, and some may not be available for local use on your Mac. Dhwani tells you when the selected language can't be used.

Does it rewrite or polish what I say?+

Dhwani turns speech into text. It doesn't use an AI writer to polish your wording, summarize, or change your tone. Read the result and make any corrections in your document or message.

How accurate is it?+

We're still testing different languages, microphones, and recording lengths. We haven't measured enough real dictations to promise an accuracy rate. Expect to make corrections, especially to names and numbers, and check the text before using it.

Can I use it without automatic paste?+

Yes. Dhwani copies your finished text, and you paste it with Command-V. Accessibility permission lets Dhwani paste into the text box where you started, as long as you haven't switched apps or text boxes. You don't need this permission for basic dictation.

Does my voice leave my Mac?+

Dhwani asks Apple's speech service to process your words on your Mac and won't switch to online dictation. We haven't independently checked internet connections during use. Read the speech and privacy details before allowing access. History starts off on a fresh installation. If you enable it, the saved text isn't encrypted by Dhwani. Updates keep your saved settings.

Why might my Mac block the download?+

This early beta hasn't gone through Apple's app verification process. If macOS blocks the first launch and you trust this download, Open Anyway may be available for Dhwani in System Settings > Privacy & Security. Some work or school Macs won't allow this. Read the installation help before starting.

What should I expect from the early beta?+

Start with short text that doesn't contain private information. Everyday dictation, automatic paste, permission recovery, long recordings, and installation on a fresh Mac still need broader testing. Updates are manual: download the new version, quit Dhwani, and replace the app in Applications. Read more about this beta.
