YOUR EVERYDAY APPS

A few words. A familiar shortcut.

  1. 01

    Choose a field

    Click where you want to write, in a document, message, or text editor.

  2. 02

    Say what you mean

    Press Option-Space to start. Speak, then press it again to finish. Option-Escape cancels.

  3. 03

    Keep writing

    Dhwani copies the transcript. With Accessibility access, it can also paste into the original field.

If you switch apps or fields, automatic paste is withheld. You can paste manually with Command-V. Live delivery across apps is still being tested.

KNOW WHAT YOU'RE USING

Apple speech.
Clearly disclosed.

Apple supplies the speech engine and language models. Dhwani requires an available on-device model and has no network-recognition fallback.

Read the full disclosure ↗

Local processing is required in source

This relies on Apple's documented API behavior. Runtime network activity has not been independently audited.

You choose what gets saved

History, the latest-transcript file, and launch at login start off. Upgrades preserve your choices.

The clipboard is part of the workflow

Regular dictation replaces the clipboard. Other apps can read it, and your destination app handles pasted text under its own policies.

Before you try it

Can I download Dhwani today?

There is no public download link yet. Private test packages were prepared before the rename. The maintainer will confirm the filename and checksum for your session. Request the early beta with your macOS version, chip family, and speech language. A GitHub account is needed, and requests are public. Do not include private dictations or contact details.

What has been tested?

Build, automated checks, packaging, and startup have passed for the reviewed versions. Live dictation, paste, privacy observations, and first installation on a fresh Mac still need acceptance testing. Read the verification record for exact checks and their limits.

Do I need to give Accessibility access?

Only if you want automatic paste. Microphone and Speech Recognition access are needed to dictate. Without Accessibility, Dhwani copies the transcript and you paste it with Command-V.

Can I contribute or build it myself?

Yes. The app source is MIT licensed. Start with the contributor guide or build instructions. Useful work includes setup, permission recovery, languages, and safe text delivery.
